Lag-1 (HE_Pct_lag1, FE_Pct_lag1)
I decided to include a lag-1 feature because what happened last year clearly shapes what happens this year. Schools tweak their advice, universities shift their entry standards, and families look at what the previous cohort did when making decisions. If I just fed each year into the model as a standalone observation, I'd be ignoring that connection entirely. Plotting an ACF on the HE percentage would almost certainly show significant correlation at lag 1, which backs up the idea that last year's figure is a useful predictor. Without the lag, the model is essentially blind to whether it's looking at a year that followed strong or weak HE uptake.
Covid Regime Dummy
I added this because there's a clear structural break in the data. From 2004/05 through to 2018/19, the HE percentage for girls sat in a fairly steady range of about 44–51%. Then in 2019/20 it shot up to 55.8% and peaked at 56.3% the following year. That wasn't because girls suddenly became more qualified — it was grade inflation from teacher-assessed grades during COVID. If I let the model try to draw one trend line through both periods, it would either chase the spike or flatten out the pre-COVID pattern. A simple binary flag lets the model treat these as two different contexts, which is a pretty standard thing to do in econometrics when you know an external event has disrupted the normal pattern.
Momentum (HE_Momentum, FE_Momentum)
I wanted something that picked up on the direction things were moving, not just the level. A HE percentage sitting at 50% tells a different story depending on whether it came off the back of 48% the year before or dropped down from 55%. Momentum captures that. I found it especially useful here because after the COVID spike, the HE percentage fell back from 56.3% to 51% and then 49.1% — there's a clear correction happening, and the momentum feature puts a number on how fast that pullback is. For a random forest, that kind of signal matters because the algorithm is splitting on values, and there's a big difference between a momentum of -5.3 and +1.1 when it comes to predicting where things go next.
